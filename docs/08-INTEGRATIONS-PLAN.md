# 08 — Integrations Plan

> Historical planning snapshot. For current operational and approval truth, use [`docs/integrations/README.md`](integrations/README.md). Important correction effective from the 9 December 2025 UIDAI amendment: an entity performing Aadhaar Paperless Offline e-KYC or Aadhaar App verifiable-credential verification must register as an OVSE. Earlier “zero-licence” wording below has been superseded.

Source: `docs/research/01-integrations-india-dpi.md` (all section numbers below refer to it unless noted). Every provider is coded against `packages/providers` interfaces (`docs/02-ARCHITECTURE.md` §5) — mock is the default env everywhere; nothing here calls a live URL from a test.

## 2. DPDP Act 2025 compliance checklist → product features

Rules notified 13–14 Nov 2025 (§12); phased commencement — Phase 2 (14 Nov 2026) opens Consent Manager registration, Phase 3 (14 May 2027) activates the bulk of substantive obligations. **Build to Phase-3 spec now**, not the interim grace period.

| DPDP requirement | Detail (§12) | ApplyOnce feature it maps to |
|---|---|---|
| Itemised consent notice (Rule 3) | Itemised by data category, specific purpose per item, plain language, stand-alone (not buried in T&Cs), equally-easy withdrawal | `ConsentSheet` component (`docs/04-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` §5) — field list grouped by section with per-field source; "Share" and "Decline" equally prominent; revoke is one tap in `/app/connections` |
| Data Principal rights | Access, correction/erasure, grievance redressal + Board escalation, nomination on death/incapacity | `POST /me/export`, `POST /me/erase` (30-day grace, legal holds), `GET /me/audit` (`docs/05-API-AND-FLOWS.md` §1); `/dpo` page for grievance intake; `family.nominee.*` doubles as the DPDP nomination record |
| Children's data / verifiable parental consent | Child = under 18; no behavioural monitoring/targeted ads at children; **Rule 10 explicitly names DigiLocker-based identity verification** as an acceptable way to confirm the parent is an identifiable adult with a valid parent-child relationship | `relations(basis='minor')` guardian flow reuses this exact pattern: a guardian's own DigiLocker/Aadhaar-offline verification is the proof of parenthood, not a bespoke age-gate. Ward's own consent required at 18 (handover flow, F5) |
| Breach notification (Rule 7) | Two-stage: notify the Board "without delay," then a detailed report within **72 hours**; stacks on top of CERT-In's existing **6-hour** mandate — dual-track applies | Breach runbook in `docs/` (per `docs/00-README.md`'s honest-constraints note); on-call rotation + incident template with both clocks tracked; audit_log's hash-chained rows are the evidence trail for any report |
| Consent Manager registration | India-incorporated, ₹2 crore minimum net worth, structurally independent from any Data Fiduciary/Processor role for the same individual, 7-year consent-record retention, registration window opens **14 Nov 2026**, penalties up to ₹50 crore | ApplyOnce positions as a **Data Fiduciary integrating with a registered Consent Manager** (not registering as one itself) for v1 — cheaper and faster; revisit CM registration only once volume justifies the ₹2cr net-worth bar and structural-independence constraint |
| Significant Data Fiduciary readiness | SDF designation by volume/sensitivity/security-risk; obligations include an India-based DPO reporting to the Board, an independent data auditor, annual DPIA | Given ApplyOnce's breadth (identity+education+income+health+bank), build SDF-grade governance pre-emptively: `/dpo` contact page now, DPO role in the admin console (`docs/02-ARCHITECTURE.md` §6), annual audit as a P2/P3 checklist item |
| Adjacent regimes (RBI KYC MD 2016, IT Act §43A/SPDI Rules) | Remain in force until DPDP Phase 3 repeals them (14 May 2027); ApplyOnce is a facts *provider*, not a substitute for a regulated entity's own CDD | Partner console never claims to satisfy a bank's own KYC obligation — payload is "verified facts with provenance," the receiving RE still runs its own CDD |

## 3. 12-month provider roadmap

**Months 0–3 (P1).** Keep synthetic provider fixtures for demos; apply for DigiLocker Requester access and UIDAI OVSE registration in parallel after legal-entity setup. Do not accept real Aadhaar offline credentials until OVSE registration is confirmed. Ship the browser extension MVP without bypassing any portal control. Start the Sub-AUA/KUA partner conversation only if online Aadhaar auth becomes a justified near-term need.

**Months 3–9 (P2).** Swap mock → Setu sandbox → Setu live for DigiLocker + Aadhaar offline XML + PAN + e-Sign — ApplyOnce's first real-provider integration. Add one KYC-aggregator sandbox (IDfy or Decentro) for CKYC search/download and penny-drop bank verification. Begin the Sahamati AA-FIU certification conversation in parallel (white-label under an existing FIU, since direct FIU registration is closed to ApplyOnce). Add DigiLocker pull for the 3 confirmed e-District states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu); manual-upload+OCR fallback everywhere else.

**Months 9–18 (P3).** ABDM HIU exploration (M1→M3 certification) only if the health vertical shows traction. Formal DPDP Consent Manager registration decision once Phase 2 rules are live (14 Nov 2026) and ApplyOnce has volume to justify it — otherwise stay a Data Fiduciary integrating with a registered CM. Pursue an Entity-Locker/API-Setu-style pilot MoU with one state government portal as public validation. Do not depend on CKYC direct access, CBDT-as-AA-FIP for ITR, UMANG as a third-party API, or UDID/CoWIN DigiLocker pulls as sole sources — all four keep a manual-upload+OCR fallback permanently, not just until year one.

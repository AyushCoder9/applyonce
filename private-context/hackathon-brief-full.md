# Full builder brief retained for private project history

## The challenge

Pick one real problem you have faced on an Indian public-service website or digital service. Then build a simpler, clearer and more useful way to solve it.

You could rethink a service related to travel, taxes, pensions, certificates, payments, grievances or any other public need. IRCTC, EPFO and the Income Tax portal are examples, not a fixed list.

## What we want you to build

For this hackathon, your prototype should be built with Codex or powered by an OpenAI model. Codex should be a meaningful part of how you build it, not something added only for the submission. If your build is selected among the 10 finalists, OpenAI will provide support. Your prototype should solve the real problem through a complete citizen journey. Use mock data, accounts and backend behavior where production access would be unsafe or unavailable. Reviewers will test the citizen experience, not an admin panel.

Your prototype should:

- Solve one clearly defined user problem.
- Let reviewers complete the main journey from start to finish.
- Be easier to understand or use than the current experience.
- Be designed for real Indian users, including people on mobile devices, slower connections or with limited digital experience.
- Use mock or synthetic data wherever personal information, payments, OTPs or government systems would normally be involved.

## What a strong build looks like

A strong submission makes these things obvious:

- Who is facing the problem?
- What is difficult about the current experience?
- What did you change?
- Why is your version better?
- What works today, and what is still mocked?
- How could the idea work safely at a larger scale?

Show the complete citizen journey. A static design is not enough. The interface and interactions should work from start to finish, while mocked data and dependencies should be clearly identified.

## What not to do

Please do not:

- Try to access, test or interfere with a live government system.
- Reverse-engineer private systems or use undocumented private APIs.
- Scrape personal or restricted information.
- Use real Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, passwords, OTPs, payment details, health information or other sensitive personal data.
- Present your prototype as an official government product.
- Use government logos in a way that suggests approval or partnership.
- Submit an old project with only small changes.
- Include code, assets or data you do not have permission to use.

## What to submit

The Project Submission form opens at 8:00 PM IST on August 28, 2026 and closes at 10:00 PM IST on August 29, 2026, a 26-hour window. There is no grace period after the form closes.

- A live public link that opens in a browser without requesting access. Reviewers will not download a mobile app.
- Mock consumer login credentials if the project requires them.
- One video, no longer than two minutes. Use the first minute to demo the project as a citizen and the second minute to explain how it was built and why those choices were made. Both teammates may present.
- A project summary under 250 words explaining what it is and why it is better than the current solution.
- The partner’s registered email if the team has two people. Leave it blank for a solo entry. Both teammates must have registered, but one teammate submits the form for the whole team.

Make sure every link works without requesting access. To replace a submission, submit the form again before it closes. The latest response for the team is used.

## How builds will be judged

- Problem: Is this a real and important user problem?
- Working build: Does the main journey actually work?
- Usability: Is the experience simpler, clearer and more accessible?
- Product thinking: Are the choices thoughtful and well explained?
- End-to-end thinking: Does the solution address the backend, infrastructure and processes, not just the interface?
- Honesty: Are limitations, mock data and dependencies clearly disclosed?

## What happens next

Selection runs in two stages after the form closes. The same registration email is used at every step so entries can be tracked; this private repository intentionally does not store that email.

### Stage 1: shortlist of 250

Every submission received before the private form closes is reviewed by the team together with OpenAI. The top 250 builds are shortlisted, and everyone who submitted gets an email with the result.

### Stage 2: 250 to 10

The 250 receive one week of mentorship through a WhatsApp group with mentors from engineering, technology and the OpenAI team, then resubmit the improved build in the same format by September 7, 2026 using the same email addresses as before. The 10 finalists are announced between September 8 and 12. Finalists present live in Bengaluru on September 12, 2026, and winners are announced the same day.

Selection does not guarantee that a government body will adopt or implement a build. The initiative is intended to start a useful conversation about better public digital experiences.

## Frequently asked questions retained from the brief

### Registering

Registration is an expression of interest, not a confirmation of a place. Updates are shared through the email and WhatsApp details used during registration.

Participants may be developers, designers, product thinkers, students, or independent builders. Participation can be solo or in a team of two; both teammates must register separately and be at least 18 years old.

### Scope

Choose one specific problem within a public-service website or digital journey, or rethink the entire experience. A Figma design alone is not enough. Every feature demoed must work. Mock data and approved sandbox behavior are expected where production access is unsafe or unavailable.

### Tools and rules

Codex is mandatory for the submitted prototype and must be meaningfully involved. Other tools and libraries are allowed when their use is permitted and disclosed. Existing government websites may be studied to understand a problem, but their code, infrastructure, private systems, and undocumented APIs may not be copied or used.

Live government connections are not allowed unless the organizers provide a specifically approved public sandbox. Real user data, real IDs, passwords, OTPs, payments, health information and other sensitive data must not be used. Official logos must not make an independent prototype appear official or endorsed.

### Submission and judging

The submission window is 8:00 PM IST August 28 to 10:00 PM IST August 29, 2026. The video is two minutes maximum: minute one is a citizen demo and minute two explains how it was built and why. One teammate submits for the team, and a replacement can be submitted before the deadline.

Visual design alone is not enough. Judges also consider whether the problem is real, whether the core journey works, whether the solution is practical, and whether mocked data and limitations are honest.

### Event

The initiative is not an official government hackathon. It is an independent builder initiative presented by Varun Mayya in partnership with OpenAI. Government or public-service representatives may be invited, but endorsement should not be assumed.

The finale is planned for September 12, 2026 in Bengaluru. Exact details are shared directly with finalists. The event is recorded and winners are announced the same day.

### Data and rights

Registration and submission data are managed by the organizer’s team, with the final privacy notice explaining access, purpose and deletion. Builders retain rights to work they own or have permission to use. Updates are shared through the registration email and WhatsApp number.

## ApplyOnce interpretation

The product selected the repeated re-entry of identity, education, family, address, income, category, certificate, and document data as its canonical citizen problem. The public demo uses synthetic data and an independent implementation. The mass-scale plan extends the same consent and provenance model to education, public services, employment, household, healthcare administration, and other structured application journeys without scraping or bypassing protected systems.
